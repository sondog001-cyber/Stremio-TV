# Origin-first access audit — all 24 missing curated channels (2026-10-12)

**Research-only companion to draft PR #83.** Last *confirmed successful deployment* is [run 38316450648](https://github.com/sondog001-cyber/Stremio-TV/actions/runs/38316450648): **87/111 published, 24 missing**. Other Actions runs may be in progress; never conflate a green *research* workflow with deployed source recovery.

## Executive finding

- **0/24** newly verified unauthenticated, directly reusable, exact-linear public HLS media origins. **0/24** newly observed progressing/direct HLS plus full FFmpeg/repeat QA passes in this pass; therefore zero candidate promotions and zero new Stremio test links. This is an absence of *verification*, not proof that no such stream exists anywhere.
- **5/24** have a broadcast-TV **OTA origin** in the Philadelphia market: KYW, WCAU, WPVI, WPHL and WPSG. Antenna reception depends on exact location/hardware. A LAN HDHomeRun offers private **direct MPEG-TS over HTTP** (not necessarily HLS) where supported; never expose a private device URL or proxy on the public addon.
- **19/24** have only official authenticated/entitled distribution pathways established by this audit (some are DTC subscriptions, some cable/live-TV providers, some provider-exclusive or event-only). An authenticated first-party app is **not** a publicly reusable, lawful Stremio stream URL.
- **Two high-confidence legacy identity aliases:** HBO Movies = old HBO Zone, and Cinemax Classics = old 5StarMax (Sept 2025 rebrands). This upgrades search terms, **not** stream-health or distribution authorization. [Rebrand report](https://www.thewrap.com/hbo-max-renames-linear-channels-rebrand/); [TV provider announcement](https://www.gta.net/product-announcements).
- **A meaningful new DTC route:** MS NOW Membership actually **launched Sept 9, 2026** with 24/7 live television; previous network FAQs saying 'launches later' are stale. [Official VERSANT release](https://www.versantmedia.com/ms-now/press-releases/become-ms-now-member-new-way-watch-connect-engage). Still paid/authenticated; no public HLS source.

## Method and confidence

Compared the exact 24 production-missing `tvg-id` values with first-party broadcaster/provider viewing, channel identities, recent channel rebranding, Philadelphia OTA licensing, and the prior [fresh-origin playlist scanner results](fresh-origin-discovery-2026-10-12.md). The previous scanner reviewed **3,168 playlist entries per batch** against **158 already known entry hosts**, finding only four speculative `greektoday.shop` links for CBSSN/ESPNews/CNBC/MS NOW; all four failed HTTPS TLS before receiving a manifest. No private accounts were signed in, no encrypted DRM session was captured, no rebroadcast was attempted, and no claim of direct origin video decode is made here.

**Terminology:** 'Provider' indicates a verified official viewing route, *not* project-eligible direct HLS. 'OTA' indicates full linear original radio-frequency broadcast and a plausible private LAN-tuner architecture; reception and Stremio codec compatibility remain untested. 'No public HLS verified' must not be interpreted as proof the feed does not exist.

## Proof gate before another Stremio test batch

1. Document lawful public/unrestricted original source or an explicitly permitted user-private direct upstream; inspect service/affiliate/feed and license/terms first.
2. Validate exact full linear channel and East/West/affiliate identity against broadcaster guide; explicitly reject FAST news, clips and branded VOD substitutes.
3. Resolve HLS master to actual media, capture final manifest and **segment delivery origin**, require two advancing windows plus readable segments. Research branch already has this stricter preflight.
4. Run sustained FFmpeg decode (~60s minimum), repeat/stall detection and corroborate source continuity over multiple checks. A runner/geo block is inconclusive, not a pass.
5. Only after eligible source + identity + media QA create a separately labeled Local Test candidate. User client verification must remain distinct from automated decoder QA. Preserve production 87/111 baseline and per-URL/channel kill switches.

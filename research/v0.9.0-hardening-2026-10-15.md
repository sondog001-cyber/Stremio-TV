## Findings and isolated changes

- A bare local-verification flag could bypass failed decoder QA, including a detected repeated clip. Manual approval is now bound to the exact channel/URL/family/headers loaded from the configured manual file. All existing 19 approvals (17 TVNow) remain eligible. Other source flags and changed/copied records cannot grant the exception.
- Detected repeated/frozen/stalled playback and identical media samples cannot use an override. Runner timeouts/access failures retain the intended local mechanism; they are not proof of geography and remain failed CI evidence.
- Override QA now says `passed:false`, `publication_accepted:true`, carries `local_verified_override:true`, and preserves the complete underlying CI decoder attempts. The publication decision remains distinct from a normal pass.
- TVNow diagnostics now join exact channel and URL, preventing a passing alternate from overwriting a failed URL’s result.
- The additive `channel-readiness.json` report covers every curated ID, selected Primary/backups, candidate origins/families, decoder/frame evidence, failures, continuity, burn-in, local status, EPG status, stability and carried publication history. Untested backups remain untested. Hostname differences do not certify origin independence.
- Offline replay of the 91 recorded Primary/backup attempts yielded the same outcomes: 85 normal successes, one local override, five failures. This does not replace a new full staging build.

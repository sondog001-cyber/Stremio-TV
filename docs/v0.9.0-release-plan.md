# v0.9.0 release gates and v1.0.0 roadmap

The complete continuation instructions were received on 2026-10-15. Historical
baseline documents mentioning missing Phases 2–3 describe the earlier state.
Work resumes from completed evidence and PRs; it does not restart the project.

## Gate checklist

- At least 95 unique original curated identities published with qualified normal
  upstream/decoder evidence, correct US East/local programming and no substitutions.
- Three consecutive successful scheduled production attempts at >=95 under the
  final configuration. An intervening failure or result below 95 resets the count.
  Manual, PR and retry runs and carried site history are not substitutes.
- Disney Channel, History and National Geographic present with correct identities.
- New/replaced sources pass normal HLS/segment/continuity/burn-in, cooldown/stability,
  FFmpeg/repeat checks, repeated observations and primary/backup promotion testing.
- Explicit local exceptions stay scoped and fully reported; repeat/freeze/stall
  defects cannot be hidden, and exceptions do not count as normal CI success.
- Correct recognizable logos with consistent padding and zero generic fallbacks;
  exact tvg-id and Philadelphia affiliate mappings; valid XMLTV/native Stremio EPG
  and actual Stremio playback. No invented guide metadata.
- Successful Pages deployment, unchanged two-hour cadence/concurrency, protected
  production and rollback, and no critical unresolved regression.
- Clean documented repository and practical clean-fork/compatibility smoke tests.
- Consistent addon version, installation instructions, changelog and release body.

Keep the production floor **86** and version **0.7.0** while these gates remain
unmet. Preserve the critical identity guard and the original 111-channel roster.
Raise the floor to 95 only when ready without needlessly interrupting production.
Prepare version/release files on a branch; finalize 0.9.0 and tag/publish only
after all acceptance checks. A successful automated audit alone is insufficient.
